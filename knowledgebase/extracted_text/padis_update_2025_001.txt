Guidelines for the Prevention and Management
of Pain, Anxiety, Agitation/Sedation, Delirium,

RATIONALE: Critically ill adults are at risk for a variety of distressing and con-            (Methodology Chair)1,2,3

Medicine’s 2018 Clinical Practice Guidelines for the Prevention and Management             Molly McNett, RN, PhD, CNRN, FNCS,

posed of 24 individuals including nurses, physicians, pharmacists, physiothera-            Michelle E. Kho, PT, PhD3,10,11

                                                                                             CCRN6,16,17

framework to formulate recommendations.                                                    Anna Krupp, RN, PhD, CCNS,
